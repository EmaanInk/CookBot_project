COOKBOT: **Simplified Yeast Pancakes Recipe (Unconventional)**

Please note that this recipe is highly unconventional and the result may vary greatly. Traditional yeast pancakes require flour, sugar, and other ingredients to create a light and fluffy texture. This recipe is an experimental attempt to make pancakes with just eggs and yeast.

Ingredients:
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon active dry yeast
- Water (approximately 1/2 cup, adjust to achieve desired consistency)

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs until they become frothy.
2. Add the yeast to the eggs and whisk until well combined.
3. Gradually add water to the egg and yeast mixture, whisking continuously. The mixture should start to froth and become slightly thicker due to the yeast's activity.
4. Let the mixture sit in a warm place for about 30 minutes to an hour, allowing the yeast to activate and froth the mixture further.
5. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat.
6. Using a spoon, drop small amounts of the mixture onto the pan, trying to create a round shape.
7. Cook for about 2-3 minutes, until the bottom is lightly browned.
8. Flip the pancake and cook for another 1-2 minutes, until the other side is lightly browned.

**Expected Outcome:**
The resulting "pancakes" will likely be thin, dense, and may not have the traditional pancake texture. They might be more similar to a crepe or a thin, yeast-leavened flatbread. The taste will be eggy and potentially yeasty, depending on the yeast's activity.

**Note:**
This recipe is experimental and not recommended for a traditional pancake experience. For a better result, consider adding flour, sugar, and other ingredients typically used in yeast pancake recipes.
